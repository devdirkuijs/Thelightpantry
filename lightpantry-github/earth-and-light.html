The Studio

Built work, clearly seen

Earth & Light Visual Studio specialises in architectural aerial photography — commercial builds, site progress, and the finer detail that gives a structure its character.

We photograph from above so clients, developers, and architects can show scale and setting with composure. When the story also needs ground-level interiors or people, we work alongside The Light Pantry.
